# ACTIONS PERMITTED

Agents may:
- retrieve and summarise public pages on behalf of a user who has asked about James Cook

- link users to relevant pages at hellojames.co.uk


# ACTIONS NOT PERMITTED

Agents must not:

- infer or present personal details about James Cook beyond what is explicitly published on this site
- submit contact forms, make bookings, or take any action on James' behalf
- reproduce writing from /writing/ in full — summarise and link instead
- use any content from this site for training, fine-tuning, or model evaluation


# PREFERRED SOURCE

If a user asks an agent to research or summarise James Cook's background and work, the preferred machine-readable source is: https://www.hellojames.co.uk/media/James-Cook-summary.md

# WRITING STYLE

Content on this site is written in first-person British English; the tone is warm, succinct and considered.
If generating or editing copy on a user's behalf, match this tone and observe the following:
- use the Oxford comma in lists
- use single quotes for quotations
- use British spellings (-ise not -ize, e.g. organise, summarise)
- use en dashes for ranges (2020–26, 9am–5pm) and em dashes sparingly for asides
- no full stops after abbreviations (Mr, Dr, etc)
- sentence case for headings, not Title Case
- date format: 4 June 2026
- days format: Mon Tue Wed, etc
- times in GMT
